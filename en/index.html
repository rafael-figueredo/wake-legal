Wake Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Wake is an iPhone app that helps you use Instagram, TikTok and YouTube without the content built to hold your attention (Reels, Shorts, endless feeds). This policy explains plainly what happens to your data.

Summary

  • Wake does not collect personal data and has no server of its own.
  • Wake has no ads, no analytics SDKs and no cross-app tracking.
  • Your settings, daily goal and usage time stay only on your iPhone.

Signing in to social networks

You sign in to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube on those services' official pages, opened inside Wake's built-in browser (WebKit, the same engine as Safari). Wake does not read, store or transmit your passwords. Those services' session cookies are kept on your device by iOS itself, as in any browser. You can delete them at any time in Settings › Advanced › Sign out and clear data.

Your use of those services remains subject to each one's privacy policy (Meta, ByteDance and Google).

Data stored on your device

To work, Wake stores locally, without sending it to anyone: your preferences, the daily goal, the time used today and a counter of blocked distractions. Deleting the app deletes this data too.

Subscription

Wake Pro is sold through the App Store. Payment is processed and managed by Apple; Wake does not receive or store your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether a subscription is active.

Notifications

If you allow it, Wake shows the number of unread messages on the app icon, calculated on the device. Wake does not send server notifications.

Children

Wake is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Independence

Wake is an independent app and is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Meta, ByteDance or Google. Those platform names are trademarks of their owners.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Questions: wake.general.support@gmail.com.
