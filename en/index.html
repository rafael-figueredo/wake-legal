How it works

What matters stays.
What hooks you goes.

Instagram, just friends

Feed and Stories from people you follow, with no ads or suggestions, and an ending: “You're all caught up.” A Reel a friend sends you opens; the next one doesn't.

TikTok, only what's sent

Opens straight to your messages. Watch the video you got, and that's it: no For You page, no search, no endless scrolling.

YouTube, without Shorts

Long videos and your subscriptions stay. The Shorts shelf and tab disappear.

Daily goal

Wake adds up your time across all three apps and lets you know when you hit your goal. It doesn't block you: leaving is your call.

Strict Mode

To change your filters, Wake asks for a pause and a phrase. Long enough for the impulse to pass.

Widgets and shortcuts

Jump straight to your Instagram messages or YouTube subscriptions with one tap on the Home Screen, or with Siri.

Private by default

No sign-up, no server, no ads, no tracking. You sign in on each network's official page; Wake never reads or stores passwords.

Questions

Does Wake replace the official apps?

No. It opens each network's official web version inside the app and removes only the parts built to hold your attention. You keep using your own accounts.

Can Wake see my password or messages?

No. You sign in on each network's official page, and nothing leaves your iPhone for Wake. See the privacy policy.

Can I turn the filters off?

Reels, Shorts and the endless feed are always blocked: that's what Wake is for. Settings like video sound and Stories can be changed in Settings › Advanced.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Wake.
